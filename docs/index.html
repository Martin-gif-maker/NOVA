An AI assistant on your computer that does things — and asks before anything risky.

Open apps, find files, read your documents, remember what matters, talk in Bulgarian or English. Use Groq, Claude or OpenAI — or run it with no internet at all.

Download for Mac Windows (test version) Source code

Free and open source (MIT). The Mac app updates itself and checks every download.

What it does

NOVA gives the AI 52 tools on your computer and sends only the ones a request needs. Everything that could change or delete something waits for your yes.

Does things, not just talk

Opens apps and pages, controls volume and music, moves files to the Trash, sets reminders, drafts mail, takes screenshots.

Asks before anything risky

Shell commands, deleting, quitting apps, typing for you — an approval every time. Text from web pages can never quietly send your data anywhere.

Your documents, by meaning

Drop in PDFs, Word or Excel files. Ask in other words — or in Bulgarian about an English contract — and it finds the right passage. On your computer.

Speech in any language, offline

Whisper runs on your computer: Bulgarian, English and 97 more languages, without your voice leaving it.

Remembers you — everywhere

Memories it picks by meaning, and the same memories on your Mac and your PC through your own cloud folder, encrypted.

Runs without the internet

One click downloads a small model through Ollama. In NOVA's tests it picked the right tool for 94 % of requests.

Measured, not guessed

86 everyday requests in English and Bulgarian go through NOVA's real AI loop with stand-in tools. A request counts as correct only if the model picked the right tool and filled it in right. The set and the harness.

Privacy

  • Your messages go only to the AI service you choose — or nowhere, with a local model.
  • "What NOVA sent" lists every request that left your computer, by address.
  • Keys stay in the Keychain (Credential Manager on Windows). Chats and memories can be encrypted on disk.
  • Documents are indexed on your computer; only the passages that answer a question are sent.

Under the hood

  • Python and Qt; one code base for macOS and Windows behind a small platform layer.
  • A tool-calling agent loop with rate-limit pacing, retries and a model fallback.
  • About 650 automated tests, lint and type checks on every change.
  • On-device models: multilingual-e5 for meaning, Whisper for speech.
